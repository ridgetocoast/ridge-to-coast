What we keep

Short version: the map stores nothing about you, your settings live in your own browser, and the only personal data we hold is an email address you typed in and confirmed.

The map

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts. The content security policy blocks them at the browser level, so this is enforced rather than promised.

When you search a place or use “find me”, the request goes to the relevant public service (OpenStreetMap Nominatim for geocoding, the National Weather Service for frost, USGS for streamflow, iNaturalist for observations) to answer that one question. We do not log it, and your browser is told to send no referrer. Geolocation is only ever read after you grant it, and the coordinates stay in the page.

Your settings

Layer choices, home location, hardiness zone, and units are stored in your browser's local storage under the key r2c.prefs.v1. They never leave your device. They are not cookies and they are not readable by us. You can view, export, and erase every value on the preferences page, or clear site data in your browser.

The newsletter

If you join the list, we store:

Email
The address you entered. Used only to send the monthly note.
Zone
Optional. Chooses which planting window you get.
Status
Pending, confirmed, or unsubscribed, with the time each happened.
Consent record
A one-way hash of the IP address that submitted the form, plus the browser's user-agent string. Kept as evidence that consent was given, and to rate-limit abuse. The IP itself is not stored.

Double opt-in

Submitting the form does not subscribe you. We send one confirmation email; if you do not click the link, you get nothing further and the pending record is deleted after 30 days.

Leaving

Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link that works without logging in. It takes effect immediately. To have your record deleted outright rather than marked unsubscribed, reply to any message and ask.

Who else sees it

The list is stored in our own database. An email delivery provider processes messages on our behalf and receives your address in order to deliver them. We do not sell, rent, share, or trade the list, and there are no partner mailings.

Changes

If this note changes in a way that affects what we collect, subscribers are told in the next monthly email before it takes effect.

Ridge to Coast maps the eastern corridor from the Appalachian ridge across the Fall Line to the Atlantic. Ecoregions from the US EPA, hardiness zones from the USDA, basemap by OpenStreetMap contributors and CARTO.
