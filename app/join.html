What to do on your land this month

One email, once a month, written for this corridor. What is safe to plant in your hardiness zone, what the rivers are running, and what people are finding in your ecoregion right now.

What arrives

  • The planting window. What to start indoors, direct sow, and transplant this month — for your zone, not a national average.
  • Frost risk. When the first and last frost dates are actually trending, not the ones on the seed packet.
  • Water. What the gauges are reading on the rivers in your watershed.
  • What is out there. A few notable observations logged in your ecoregion.

No tracking pixels, no partner lists, no “we've updated our terms” mail. Unsubscribe from any message in one click. Read the privacy note — it is short.
